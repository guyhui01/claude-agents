# Skill — User Research
> Certifications: NN/g UX-C · CPUX-UR · IDF

## Objective
Collect qualitative and quantitative data about users to inform design decisions.

## Available methods
- **Semi-structured interviews**: 5 to 8 participants, interview guide, verbatims *(theme saturation drives when to stop, not a fixed quota)*
- **Contextual observations**: shadowing, think-aloud protocol *(Contextual Inquiry, Beyer & Holtzblatt 1998)*
- **Diary studies**: user journal over 7 to 14 days
- **Quantitative surveys**: Google Forms, Typeform — n ≥ 30 *(indicative statistical-robustness threshold, to be sized against the target margin of error)*
- **Guerrilla research**: ~5 minutes, public places, quick insights

## Deliverables
- Structured interview guide (warm-up / core / wrap-up)
- Observation grid
- Insights synthesis (affinity mapping)
- Research report ready for Notion / Confluence

## Output format
Specify: desired method · number of participants · deadline · product context

## Sources
- **Hugh Beyer & Karen Holtzblatt** — *Contextual Design* (1998) — contextual inquiry, affinity mapping
- **Steve Portigal** — *Interviewing Users* (Rosenfeld Media, 2nd ed. 2023) — conducting interviews
- **Erika Hall** — *Just Enough Research* (A Book Apart, 2nd ed. 2019) — scoping and right-sizing research
- **Jakob Nielsen** — *Why You Only Need to Test with 5 Users* (NN/g, 2000) — apply with nuance
- **Tomer Sharon** — *Validating Product Ideas* (Rosenfeld Media, 2016)

## Anti-patterns
- Closed or leading questions that elicit the desired answer
- "Decorative" research run after decisions are already made
- Generalizing from a sample that is not representative of the target
- Confusing generative research (explore) with evaluative research (test a solution)
- Insights left unsynthesized / unshared → research with no impact on decisions

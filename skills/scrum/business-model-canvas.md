# Skill — Business Model Canvas & Value Proposition Canvas
> Certifications: PSPO II · PSPO III · ICAgile ICP-APO

## Objective
Model the product's business model and align the value proposition with customers' real needs.

## Business Model Canvas (Osterwalder)
9 blocks:
1. **Customer segments**: who are our customers?
2. **Value proposition**: what value do we create?
3. **Channels**: how do we reach customers?
4. **Customer relationships**: what type of relationship?
5. **Revenue streams**: how do we monetize?
6. **Key resources**: what assets are required?
7. **Key activities**: which activities are critical?
8. **Key partners**: who are our partners?
9. **Cost structure**: what are our costs?

## Value Proposition Canvas
- **Customer profile**: jobs · pains · gains
- **Value map**: products/services · pain relievers · gain creators
- Fit = when the value matches the customer's jobs/pains/gains exactly

## Use in a PO context
- Before building: validate the Problem-Solution fit
- During PI Planning: align Features with the value proposition
- In product review: demonstrate the business value delivered

## Deliverables
- Completed BMC (Figma, Miro or Notion)
- Value Proposition Canvas per customer segment
- Gap analysis: current value vs. target value

## Output format
Specify: product type (B2B, B2C, marketplace) · maturity stage · known customer segments

# Skill — Market Analysis and Competitive Positioning
> Certifications: PMI-PBA (PMI), SAFe POPM 6 (Scaled Agile), PMP (PMI)

## Objective
Analyze the market and the competitive landscape at the Program level — TAM/SAM/SOM, competitor benchmark, product SWOT, differentiating positioning — to feed the product vision and prioritization decisions.

## TAM / SAM / SOM — Template

```
TAM (Total Addressable Market)
  Total size of the global / national market
  E.g. HR AI software market — €15B in Europe (2026)

SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)
  Share of the market you can address
  E.g. Large French groups > 1000 employees — €2.5B

SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
  Share realistically capturable in 3 years
  E.g. 3-5% of SAM in 3 years → €75-125M
```

# Skill — AI Newsletter & Email Marketing
> Certifications: HubSpot Email Marketing · HubSpot Content Marketing

## Objective
Design and produce high-performing newsletters and email campaigns, personalized by AI to maximize engagement and conversions.

## Anatomy of a high-performing newsletter
```
[Subject]     → 40-60 characters, personalized, curiosity or value
[Preheader]   → 85-100 characters, complements the subject

[Header]      → Logo + newsletter title
[Hook]        → 1-2 sentences that justify reading
[Body]        → 3 sections max (short text + visual)
[CTA]         → 1 button only, clear action
[Footer]      → Legal links, unsubscribe, social media
```

## Subject-line formulas that convert
| Formula | Example | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Number** | "5 AI tools I use every day" | Lists, tops |
| **Question** | "Is your team ready for the AI Act?" | Engagement, problem |
| **Curiosity** | "What nobody tells you about LLMs" | Thought leadership |
| **Urgency** | "Last day: free AI webinar" | Event, offer |
| **Personalization** | "[First name], your May AI roadmap" | Automation, nurturing |

## AI email marketing prompt
```
"Write a [type: welcome / nurturing / promotion / re-engagement] email
for [audience: new subscribers / hot leads / inactive customers].
Objective: [expected action].
Tone: [professional / conversational / expert].
Length: [150-300 words].
Include:
  - Catchy subject + preheader
  - Personalized hook
  - Main value (1 strong idea)
  - A single, clear CTA"
```

## Automated email sequences (types)
| Sequence | Emails | Duration | Objective |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | 5 emails | D0, D1, D3, D7, D14 | Activation |
| Lead nurturing | 4 emails | D0, D3, D7, D14 | Qualification |
| Re-engagement | 3 emails | D0, D7, D14 | Win-back |
| Post-purchase | 3 emails | D0, D3, D14 | Satisfaction + upsell |

## Email KPIs to watch
> ⚠️ **Indicative ballpark figures**, to recalibrate by tool, industry, and list quality. Open rates are strongly inflated since Apple Mail Privacy Protection (automatic image preloading) — favor the **CTOR (click-to-open rate)** as a reliable engagement signal. Up-to-date reference sources: Mailchimp / HubSpot / Brevo benchmarks (2025).

| KPI | B2B ballpark | B2C ballpark |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate *(Apple MPP-biased)* | 30-45% | 30-40% |
| Click rate | 2-4% | 1-3% |
| Conversion rate | 1-3% | 0.5-2% |
| Unsubscribe rate | < 0.5% | < 0.5% |

## Deliverables
- Email templates by type (welcome, nurturing, promo)
- Complete automated sequence (5 emails)
- Audience segmentation plan
- Performance report with A/B recommendations

## Output format
Specify: email type · audience · objective · tool (Mailchimp, HubSpot, Brevo) · tone · legal constraints (GDPR)

## Anti-patterns
- ❌ **Benchmark presented as certainty** — showing a "target" open rate without framing it as a ballpark or citing a source → unrealistic goals, and Apple MPP bias ignored.
- ❌ **Competing CTAs** — multiplying buttons → dilution, drop in click rate. One email = one main CTA.
- ❌ **Misleading subject (clickbait)** — promise not kept in the body → unsubscribes and lost trust.
- ❌ **No segmentation** — same message to the whole list → low relevance, degraded deliverability.
- ❌ **GDPR neglected** — no explicit consent, no clear unsubscribe link, or no legal notices → non-compliance (GDPR, ePrivacy).
